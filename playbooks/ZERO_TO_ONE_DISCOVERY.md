# Zero-to-One Discovery Playbook

## Objective
Convert a rough idea into an evidence-backed venture specification with the minimum founder input required to start execution.

## Minimum viable input
Accept as little as:

> Business idea: [plain-English idea]

Infer the rest when possible. Ask questions only when the answer materially changes the business, creates legal/regulatory risk, or blocks execution.

## 1. Translate the idea into a venture thesis
Produce:

- customer
- problem
- current alternative
- proposed solution
- revenue model
- why now
- acquisition hypothesis
- fulfillment hypothesis
- expected gross-margin structure
- primary risk

## 2. Validate the problem before polishing the solution
Research:

- evidence of demand
- search/commercial intent
- competitor density
- pricing norms
- customer complaints
- substitutes/DIY behavior
- market growth or decline where material
- regulatory constraints

Separate:

- FACTS
- ESTIMATES
- ASSUMPTIONS
- UNKNOWN / `[PLACEHOLDER]`

## 3. Define the ICP
At minimum:

- buyer/user
- geography
- company/customer type
- trigger event
- pain severity
- willingness/ability to pay
- existing alternative
- disqualifiers

## 4. Define the zero-to-one test
Before building, state what would count as evidence that the venture deserves more investment.

Examples:

- first 5 paying customers
- 10 qualified demos
- 20% quote-to-book rate
- CAC below a defined ceiling
- 3 partner agreements

## 5. Opportunity score
Score 1–10:

- value / market opportunity
- customer pain
- probability of demand
- speed to first cash
- gross margin
- recurrence
- capital intensity
- execution complexity
- differentiation
- scalability

Identify one fatal-risk candidate and test it first.

## 6. Stop conditions
Do not proceed blindly if evidence shows:

- structurally negative unit economics
- illegal or materially non-compliant model
- no reachable customer
- impossible fulfillment dependency
- severe trademark/name conflict
- founder constraint incompatible with the model

Instead modify the model and continue with the best viable version.

## Output

A one-page venture specification that downstream Venture Launch OS phases can execute without rediscovering the business from scratch.
