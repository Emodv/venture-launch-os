# Outreach Playbook

## Objective
Create revenue, distribution, fulfillment capacity, referrals, or authority through targeted outbound.

## Prioritization
Score each target 1–10 on:

- economic value
- probability of fit
- urgency/intent signal
- decision-maker accessibility
- speed to outcome
- effort

Work highest expected value first.

## Target classes

- customers/prospects
- referral partners
- fulfillment partners
- associations/directories
- media/editorial targets
- strategic distribution partners

## Message structure
Keep outreach short:

1. Relevant observation or trigger.
2. Specific value proposition.
3. Evidence or credible reason to engage.
4. One low-friction CTA.

Avoid fake personalization, invented familiarity, or unsupported performance claims.

## Sequences
Default B2B sequence:

- Touch 1: direct value proposition.
- Touch 2: useful proof/resource or sharper angle.
- Touch 3: concise close-the-loop message.

Stop when the contact opts out or the opportunity is clearly not relevant.

## Tracking fields
Maintain:

- company/contact
- role
- source
- ICP/partner type
- trigger/intent signal
- value score
- status
- last touch
- next touch
- response
- opportunity value
- outcome/revenue

## Optimization
Judge outreach by:

1. Positive replies.
2. Qualified conversations.
3. Meetings/opportunities.
4. Won revenue or measurable partner value.

Open and click rates are diagnostics, not primary business outcomes.

## Compounding asset
Every outreach cycle should improve a reusable database of:

- responsive segments
- winning angles
- objections
- conversion rates
- partner economics
- relationship history
