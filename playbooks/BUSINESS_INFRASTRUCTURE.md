# Business Infrastructure Playbook

## Objective
Ensure the venture can legally, financially, and operationally accept customers rather than merely having a live website.

## Principle
Requirements vary by jurisdiction and business model. Research current local requirements when material. Never present legal, tax, insurance, or licensing assumptions as confirmed facts.

## Business setup checklist
Assess whether the venture needs:

- corporation / sole proprietorship / partnership decision
- business/operating name registration
- tax registration
- business bank account
- bookkeeping/accounting system
- invoicing
- payment processor
- merchant terms
- privacy policy
- terms of service / sales terms
- refund/cancellation policy
- cookie/consent mechanism where applicable
- licenses/permits
- insurance
- employment/contractor agreements
- vendor/provider agreements
- data-processing/privacy agreements

## Launch classification
Classify each requirement as:

- REQUIRED BEFORE FIRST CUSTOMER
- REQUIRED BEFORE SCALE
- OPTIONAL / BEST PRACTICE
- NOT APPLICABLE
- UNKNOWN — RESEARCH REQUIRED

## Payment readiness
If payments are part of the model, verify:

- payment account exists
- production credentials are configured securely
- currency is correct
- tax behavior is defined
- successful and failed payment flows work
- refunds/cancellations can be handled
- webhook/idempotency logic is safe when applicable

## Commercial documents
Where applicable prepare factual, plain-language versions of:

- proposal / scope
- service agreement
- terms
- privacy policy
- cancellation/refund terms
- partner/vendor agreement

Escalate legal review when risk warrants it.

## Email/domain trust
For business email, configure or verify when possible:

- SPF
- DKIM
- DMARC
- sending domain/reputation
- transactional sender identity
- unsubscribe/consent behavior where required

## Financial system
Minimum operating visibility should include:

- revenue
- refunds
- direct costs
- payment fees
- acquisition spend
- gross contribution
- accounts receivable when applicable
- tax liabilities where known

## Completion condition
A venture is commercially ready only when the founder can accept an order/lead/customer, fulfill it, collect money where applicable, record the transaction, and comply with known material requirements.
