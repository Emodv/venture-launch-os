# VLA 2.0 — Autonomous Business Growth Engine

VLA 2.0 extends VLA v0.4/v0.5. It does not replace Venture Launch OS, Venture State, privacy rules, model routing, approval classes, or the existing agent-economy layer.

## Product definition

VLA 2.0 is a persistent autonomous business-growth agent that owns the customer-acquisition and growth lifecycle across human and AI-agent discovery environments.

It is designed to help a business become:

**discoverable → understandable → trustworthy → recommendable → negotiable → transactable → measurable → continuously improving**

## Three missions

### Mission 1 — Transform an existing business

Input: an existing business URL plus authorized first-party data when available.

Pipeline:

`audit → preservation map → audience/intent intelligence → SEO/AEO/GEO/AAO modernization → upgrade/rebuild decision → agent-commerce enablement → continuous operation`

Rules:

- preserve proven equity before replacing it
- do not claim historical performance without authorized evidence
- no passwords/raw credentials
- large migrations require redirect/canonical/analytics/conversion preservation
- agent-commerce actions require explicit business authority

### Mission 2 — Launch a new venture

Input: a plain-English idea.

Pipeline:

`validate → audience/ICP → offer/economics → infrastructure → discovery foundation → acquisition → first qualified opportunity → customer → outcome learning`

The launch objective is not website completion. It is a measurable operating system capable of customer acquisition and fulfillment.

### Mission 3 — Operate the growth system

VLA coordinates growth across:

- audience intelligence
- PPC
- SEO
- AEO
- GEO
- AAO
- content
- email
- social
- CRO
- analytics/attribution
- CRM/lead routing
- experiments
- agent-to-agent discovery/evaluation/negotiation

No single channel is treated as the business objective. VLA optimizes the customer's decision environment and contribution economics.

## Autonomous team

VLA remains the persistent primary agent. VLA 2.0 adds governed specialist roles beneath the Director:

- Director — outcome ownership, strategy, budget, prioritization, governance
- Strategist — market, audience, ICP, positioning, offer, economics
- Producer — content/pages/publishing operations
- Growth Marketer — SEO/AEO/GEO/AAO/PPC/email/social/CRO
- Creative — brand, messaging, copy, creative concepts
- Analyst — attribution, ROI, experiments, benchmarks, bottlenecks
- Operations — CRM, integrations, deployment, routing, payments, fulfillment, QA

Specialists are roles/capabilities inside VLA, not independent sources of truth. All use the same Venture State, privacy rules, approvals, Decision Memory and Outcome Memory.

## Heartbeat model

A heartbeat is a governed scheduling primitive:

1. Read Venture State and work queue.
2. Mark dependency-satisfied items ready.
3. Prioritize by economic value, probability, speed and effort.
4. Route Class A/B work to the relevant specialist when authorized.
5. Route Class C work to the Director/owner for approval.
6. Execute through connected tools.
7. Verify result.
8. Persist evidence and outcome.
9. Recompute bottleneck and next priorities.

A heartbeat does not itself grant authority. Tool permissions, approval classes, budgets, security rules and provider credentials still control execution.

## Priority model

Use:

`priority = value × probability × speed ÷ effort`

Higher `speed` means faster time-to-impact, so it increases priority. Fatal constraints override the score.

## Cheap-listener model routing

Continuous monitoring should prefer the cheapest model/tool that can reliably classify the signal. Escalate when the expected value or uncertainty warrants stronger reasoning.

Routing considerations:

- capability/tool requirements
- quality benchmark
- latency
- cost
- privacy/data policy
- risk/irreversibility

Do not assume one named provider is always the cheap or premium option; benchmark current models and prices.

## AI Agent Optimization (AAO)

AAO makes a business legible and actionable to autonomous agents. VLA should expose truthful, current information about:

- entity/category
- products/services/use cases
- geography/service area
- pricing/price rules
- availability/capacity when applicable
- refund/cancellation/warranty policy
- response times
- verified evidence/provenance
- eligibility/constraints
- transaction methods/actions

VLA tracks internal agent readiness but must not claim a universal external AI-agent ranking unless a specific platform exposes one.

## Agent commerce

The canonical journey is:

`research → compare → evaluate → negotiate → authorized action → transact → fulfillment → verified outcome`

VLA asks another agent for decision-relevant principal intent and constraints, not unnecessary personal identity.

Negotiation is deterministic and policy-bound. VLA may not invent discounts, refunds, promises, contracts or transaction authority.

## Third-party capability policy

VLA may integrate external MCP servers, skills, agent frameworks, reputation networks and marketing tools, but they are adapters/capability sources only.

Before autonomous production use, require:

1. repository/source verification
2. license review
3. dependency/security review
4. data-flow/privacy review
5. pinned revision/version
6. sandbox testing
7. least-privilege credentials
8. kill switch/rollback
9. explicit mapping into VLA approval classes

No third-party project becomes the owner of Venture State, Decision Memory, Outcome Memory, privacy policy or VLA doctrine.

## Privacy moat

Historical client evidence remains private and NDA-protected. Reusable intelligence may contain only generalized, non-identifying decision/outcome patterns.

The moat is:

**conditional operating intelligence + Decision Memory + Outcome Memory + cross-venture privacy-safe learning**

not customer data.

## Success metrics

These are operating targets, not guarantees:

- reduce time to qualified opportunity
- reduce time to first paying customer
- reduce time to positive contribution economics
- increase the share of reversible tasks safely executed without human intervention
- improve source-to-revenue attribution and experiment velocity
- increase agent-readiness completeness for applicable businesses

Do not promise fixed transformation times, first-customer deadlines, ROI multiples, or universal agent-commerce readiness without evidence.

## Release strategy

VLA 2.0 should ship incrementally:

1. owned team/pipeline/heartbeat primitives
2. governed integration registry
3. persistent database and scheduler
4. authorized first-party connectors
5. production tool execution
6. A2A authentication and commerce
7. live model benchmark routing
8. privacy-safe cross-venture learning

Every stage must preserve the prior working runtime and regression suite.
