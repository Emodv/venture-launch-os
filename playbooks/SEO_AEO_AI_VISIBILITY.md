# SEO + AEO + AI Visibility Playbook

## Objective
Create factual, crawlable, useful content that can rank in search and be cited or understood by AI answer engines.

## 1. Establish entity clarity
Publish consistent facts for:

- business name
- category
- location/service area
- services/products
- pricing model where public
- contact/booking path
- operating constraints

Use consistent naming across website, schema, directories, profiles, and machine-readable files.

## 2. Technical baseline
Verify:

- production HTTPS
- one canonical URL per page
- unique title and meta description
- logical H1/H2 structure
- sitemap.xml
- robots.txt
- internal links
- mobile performance
- indexable public pages
- no accidental staging/noindex directives

## 3. Content architecture
Prioritize pages with commercial or authority value:

1. Core service/product pages.
2. Geography pages where real service exists or a clearly labelled waitlist is supported.
3. Pricing/quote guides.
4. Comparison and alternative pages.
5. FAQs answering real operational questions.
6. Proprietary calculators/data/resources.

Avoid thin doorway pages and templated filler.

## 4. Structured data
Use only schema supported by visible facts:

- Organization / LocalBusiness
- Service
- Offer
- FAQPage
- WebSite
- BreadcrumbList

Never manufacture reviews, ratings, certifications, locations, offers, or availability.

## 5. AI discovery layer
Implement the reusable template in `templates/AI_DISCOVERY_TEMPLATE.md`.

The goal is not to manipulate LLMs. The goal is to reduce ambiguity about what the business is, what it offers, where it operates, and which facts are safe to state.

## 6. Citation-worthy assets
Build assets worth referencing:

- calculators
- local pricing benchmarks
- market data
- comparison tables
- original surveys
- directories
- checklists/process guides

## 7. Off-page authority
Prioritize:

1. Google Business Profile where eligible.
2. Municipality/chamber/industry citations.
3. Real partner links.
4. Local editorial/PR coverage.
5. Review platforms customers actually use.
6. Relevant associations.

Avoid PBNs, paid spam links, and mass directory blasts.

## 8. Measurement
Track:

- indexed pages
- organic impressions/clicks
- non-brand queries
- local visibility
- referring domains
- AI referral traffic where measurable
- leads and won customers from organic sources
- pages cited/referenced by external sources

## Launch gate
SEO/AEO work is not complete until production URLs, structured files, and critical pages are tested live and revenue attribution events exist.
