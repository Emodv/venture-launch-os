# PPC Validation Playbook

## Objective
Use paid acquisition to test demand and economics, not to buy vanity traffic.

## Preconditions
Do not launch until these exist:

- clear offer
- defined ICP/geography
- conversion-focused landing page
- working lead/booking event
- source/UTM attribution
- CAC ceiling from unit economics
- explicit kill criteria

## Campaign structure
Start narrow.

### Search
Prioritize:

1. Exact/high-intent service terms.
2. Location + service terms.
3. Problem/need terms with clear commercial intent.
4. Competitor terms only when legal, relevant, and economically justified.

Avoid broad informational traffic until conversion economics are proven.

### Paid social
Use when the offer is understandable without active search intent or when local reach/retargeting matters.

Test:

- pain-led angle
- outcome-led angle
- risk-reversal/offer angle

## Landing page rule
Match ad intent to page intent.

Every page should answer:

- what is offered
- where it is available
- price/quote path
- why choose this option
- next action

## Measurement hierarchy
Optimize in this order:

1. Won customers / revenue.
2. Booked or sales-qualified opportunities.
3. Qualified leads.
4. Quote completions.
5. Clicks only as a diagnostic metric.

## Kill criteria
Before launch define thresholds for:

- spend without a qualified lead
- spend without a won customer
- maximum CAC
- minimum conversion rate
- unacceptable lead quality

Do not keep weak campaigns alive because they generate clicks.

## Scaling rule
Scale only when:

- tracking is trustworthy
- contribution margin remains positive
- lead quality is stable
- fulfillment can absorb demand

Increase budget gradually and watch marginal CAC, not only blended CAC.

## Weekly output
Report:

- spend
- qualified leads
- won customers
- revenue
- CAC
- contribution margin after ad spend
- best query/audience
- worst waste source
- one action to improve economics
